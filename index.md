I'm a PhD candidate in the computer science department at Brown University studying the evaluation of AI systems, with an emphasis on real world settings. Prior to starting my PhD, I was a data scientist working with organizations such as DARPA and McKinsey, and a open source core-maintainer at [Project Jupyter](https://jupyter.org/). My work evaluating large multilingual-language models has received the [Mozilla Technology Fund 2023 Award](https://foundation.mozilla.org/en/blog/evaluation-harness-is-setting-the-benchmark-for-auditing-large-language-models/) and has also been covered in [WIRED magazine](https://www.wired.com/story/chatgpt-non-english-languages-ai-revolution/).

Email me at jessica_forde at brown.edu.
